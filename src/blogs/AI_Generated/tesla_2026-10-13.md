==============================

Developed by researchers at Google, TESLA is designed to address some of the limitations of traditional deep learning models, which can be computationally expensive and difficult to train. Overview of TESLA
-------------------

By using tensors, TESLA can efficiently store and manipulate large amounts of data, making it well-suited for large-scale deep learning tasks.
One of the key features of TESLA is its ability to perform computations in parallel across multiple GPUs or other parallel computing devices. This allows TESLA to scale to much larger datasets and more complex models than traditional deep learning architectures.
Another important aspect of TESLA is its use of a novel optimization algorithm called the "Tensor Train" algorithm. This algorithm allows TESLA to efficiently train deep neural networks by breaking them down into smaller, more manageable pieces that can be trained in parallel.
