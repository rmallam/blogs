### Applications of Computer Vision

Computer vision has numerous applications in various industries, including:

#### Healthcare

Computer vision can be used in healthcare to analyze medical images, such as X-rays and MRIs, to diagnose and treat diseases. For example, CNNs can be used to detect tumors in medical images with high accuracy.

#### Retail

Computer vision can be used in retail to analyze customer behavior, such as tracking foot traffic and analyzing customer demographics. This can help retailers optimize their store layouts and marketing strategies.

#### Security

Computer vision can be used in security to detect and track people within surveillance footage. This can help identify potential threats and improve public safety.
